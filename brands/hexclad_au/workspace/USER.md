# USER.md -- Brand Context

## Brand Overview
- **Brand:** HexClad
- **Market:** Australia
- **Industry:** Premium Cookware
- **Website:** hexclad.com.au
- **Tagline:** The last pan you'll ever buy.

## Products
- Hybrid stainless steel + non-stick cookware
- Hero product: 12" Hybrid Pan
- Range: pans, woks, pots, knife sets, accessories
- Price point: Premium ($150-$500+ AUD per piece)
- USP: Patented laser-etched hexagonal design combines stainless steel durability with non-stick convenience
- Celebrity endorsement: Gordon Ramsay (global ambassador)

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Voice:** Confident, practical, premium but not pretentious
- **Tone:** Like a knowledgeable friend who genuinely loves cooking -- not a luxury brand lecture
- **Avoid:** Overly formal language, generic "premium lifestyle" fluff, talking down to home cooks
- **Embrace:** Real cooking scenarios, honest performance claims, the joy of cooking well

## Target Audience
- Home cooks who care about quality (not just professional chefs)
- Age: 28-55, skewing 30-45
- Income: Middle-to-upper disposable income
- Values: Quality over quantity, cooking as pleasure not chore, willing to invest in tools
- Australian market focus (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane primarily)

## Creator Criteria -- What Makes a Good Fit
- **Ideal niches:** Food, cooking, recipe content, kitchen/home, lifestyle with cooking angle
- **Follower range:** 10K-500K (sweet spot: 25K-150K for AU market)
- **Must-haves:**
  - Actually cooks (not just food photography)
  - Australian-based or AU-focused audience
  - Authentic engagement (real comments, not bot-heavy)
  - Visual quality that shows product well (kitchen/cooking content)
- **Nice-to-haves:**
  - Previous brand partnerships (shows professionalism)
  - Video content (reels/stories showing cooking process)
  - Engaged comment section with cooking questions
- **Red flags:**
  - Competitor partnerships (Le Creuset, Scanpan, All-Clad, Tefal ambassador)
  - Low engagement rate (<1.5%)
  - Inconsistent posting (gaps >2 weeks)
  - Content that doesn't feature actual cooking

## Competitors to Monitor
- Le Creuset, Scanpan, All-Clad, Tefal, Staub, Lodge
- Note: Creators with casual one-off competitor posts are fine; exclusive ambassador deals are not

## Campaign Context
- Launch phase: Building initial AU creator network
- Priority: Identify 20-30 potential partners across micro (10K-50K) and mid-tier (50K-200K)
- Content type preference: Recipe content featuring HexClad, cooking process videos, honest reviews
- Budget tier: Mid-range (product seeding + modest fees, not mega-influencer budgets)

## Geographic Focus
- Primary: Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane
- Secondary: Perth, Adelaide, Gold Coast
- Open to: Regional creators with strong food content
